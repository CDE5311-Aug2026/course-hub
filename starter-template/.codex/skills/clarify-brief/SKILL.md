# Clarify before building

Read `brief.md` and `DESIGN.md` first. Do not write code or invent product decisions.

## Ask the highest-value questions

Summarise what is already clear in three short bullets. Then ask the most important unanswered question, one at a time. Prioritise:

1. Who is the primary user, and in what real situation do they use this?
2. What single task must the prototype let them complete?
3. What is explicitly out of scope?
4. What would prove the prototype is usable?
5. Which screen should be built first?
6. Which design decisions are agreed, and which need confirmation?
7. What data or privacy constraints matter?

Ask no more than seven questions. After answers, propose concise updates to `brief.md` and `DESIGN.md`, clearly separating confirmed decisions from assumptions. Wait for approval before changing files or planning code.
