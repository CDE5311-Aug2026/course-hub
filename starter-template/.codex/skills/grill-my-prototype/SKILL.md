# Constructive prototype critique

Read `brief.md`, `DESIGN.md`, and any supplied screenshots or running-app notes. Do not write code.

## Challenge the important things

Ask up to eight questions, one at a time. Be direct but helpful. Focus on:

- whether the intended user and problem are specific;
- whether the core flow solves the problem;
- what a user would do when data is missing, long, invalid, or fails;
- whether the design is understandable, accessible and responsive;
- whether any feature is unnecessary for a usable prototype;
- what evidence would show this helps the user.

After the answers, produce:

1. The three highest-risk assumptions.
2. The one feature or decision to remove, simplify or test first.
3. The next smallest experiment or design change.
4. A short verdict: ready to build, ready after clarification, or too unclear to build.

Do not praise vague ideas. Explain each concern with a concrete consequence.
